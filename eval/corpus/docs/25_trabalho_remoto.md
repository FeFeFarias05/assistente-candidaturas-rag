# Remote teamwork

## Overview

I am Ana, a software engineering student, and this project was part of a group assignment for one of my courses. The team had four people, and we were spread across different cities, so planning had to be very clear from the beginning. I worked remotely with a team in another city, using chat and weekly video calls. Our goal was to build a small project in six weeks, with shared responsibilities and regular check-ins to keep everyone aligned.

## What I did

I helped define the weekly plan, divided tasks, and kept track of deadlines. At the start, we agreed on a simple workflow: each person would finish one part before the next meeting, and I would collect updates and summarize them for the group. My main responsibilities were:

- writing the initial project outline
- organizing meeting notes after each weekly call
- reviewing the team’s progress against the schedule
- helping combine the final parts into one consistent document

I also noticed early when one teammate was falling behind because of class exams, so I helped reassign two smaller tasks to keep the project moving.

## Results

We finished the project on time, with all required parts completed in the sixth week. The final submission was accepted without major changes. Our weekly check-ins reduced confusion, and the team made fewer last-minute decisions than in previous group work. We also ended with a shared process that made it easier to understand who was doing what at any point.

## Challenges and learnings

The biggest challenge was coordinating across cities when everyone had different schedules. Sometimes messages were answered several hours later, which slowed decisions. I learned that remote teamwork works better when responsibilities are written down clearly and reviewed every week. I also learned to be more direct in updates, because short and specific messages saved time and avoided misunderstandings.
