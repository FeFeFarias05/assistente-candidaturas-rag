# Portfolio site

## Overview

I built this project in 2025 as my personal portfolio site, with the goal of presenting my studies, projects, and contact information in a simple place. I wanted a site that felt personal but still professional, and I used the project as a chance to practice planning, implementation, and deployment on my own. I also used it to improve how I structure a small product from start to finish.

## What I did

I built my portfolio site with Vite, React and TypeScript

I started by defining the content structure and writing the main sections before coding the interface. I kept the site focused on a few pages and made the layout responsive so it would work well on mobile and desktop. I also wrote the copy myself, choosing short descriptions for my projects and a direct summary of who I am as a Software Engineering student.

I deployed the site on Vercel

During development, I organized the work in weekly steps over about three weeks:
- first week: layout and navigation
- second week: content and visual adjustments
- third week: testing and deployment

## Results

The finished site gave me a place to share my work in a clear and consistent format. I published it with the main sections I needed, and the final version was ready before the end of the month I had planned for it. It also helped me create a portfolio I can update over time as I complete new projects and internships.

## Challenges and learnings

The main challenge was keeping the site simple without making it feel empty. I had to decide what to include and what to leave out, especially on the homepage. Another difficulty was adjusting spacing and text length so the content stayed readable on different screen sizes. I also learned that planning content first saves time later, because it reduces layout changes during implementation.

From this project, I learned to:
- define a small scope and keep it stable
- review details early instead of waiting until the end
- balance design choices with maintenance needs
